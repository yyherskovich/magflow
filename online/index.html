Common questions

PublicationFlow Online, explained.

Is PublicationFlow available online?

Yes. PublicationFlow is available as an online publishing workspace as well as a desktop edition. Manage ad bookings, customers, flatplans, invoices, collections and reports directly in your browser.

Do I need Excel to use the online workspace?

No. Browser-based bookings, customer records, flatplans, billing and reports can be used in the online workspace. Existing desktop customers can use a connected setup to synchronize supported issue, customer and payment data.

How does online planning connect to InDesign?

Plan and review your issue online. Production InDesign file generation uses the desktop production workflow and accessible original artwork. Contact us to arrange the right online and desktop setup for your publication.

Where is artwork stored?

The online flatplan can show small artwork previews. When you connect a local artwork folder, original production files stay in that folder; only reduced-size previews are uploaded. Final desktop production needs access to the original files.

How do I try the online edition?

Click Start Free Trial, complete the short form, and select Open Your Free Trial on the confirmation page. The online demo opens immediately. No credit card is required.

How do I arrange an online account for my publication?

Contact PublicationFlow at info@publicationflow.com or 718-705-4939. We will help review your ad sizes, issue setup, existing data, email and payment requirements, and confirm the right configuration and pricing.
